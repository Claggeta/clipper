# Clipper v0.8.0

Eighth release, aimed at posting better Shorts. A YouTube Studio export
can now be read to show how posted Shorts do, linked to their drafts.
Drafts open on a short hook burned on screen and have their pauses cut.
The review page is simpler: one bar of controls above the film strip, a
bin beside the timeline, and a library of saved clips from any video to
splice from. A new command hands clips to DaVinci Resolve (free) as an
editable timeline. Six review fixes cut clicks per clip. Shorter windows,
a cold open and a ranker rerun were measured and not adopted. Everything
still runs locally and costs nothing.

## What it does

- **How posted Shorts do**
  ([ADR 0027](../decisions/0027-performance-data-from-studio-csv-exports.md)
  amendments, #273).
  - `clipper performance <export folder>` reads a YouTube Studio content
    export (Table data.csv) by column name, offline: no YouTube login or
    API.
  - A copy is kept under `data/performance/<last day>/`, not committed.
  - Each Short is linked to its draft by `data/posts.json`, else by title,
    length (within 2 s) and day; `--link` records the matches in
    `posts.json`.
  - Views, stayed to watch and average percentage viewed per Short, and
    medians by length, channel and style.
- **A screen hook on every draft**
  ([ADR 0018](../decisions/0018-suggested-titles-via-ollama.md) and
  [0030](../decisions/0030-on-screen-text-and-thumbnails.md)
  amendments, #275).
  - The title request also asks for a hook of at most 6 words, burned
    at the top for the clip's first 3 s, with or without captions.
  - In `review`, the first text starts as it.
- **Pauses cut in drafts**
  ([ADR 0029](../decisions/0029-cut-pauses-in-review.md) amendment,
  #277).
  - `drafts` cuts each clip's pauses in speech, as Cut silence does in
    review, unless that would remove over 35% of the clip (a moment seen
    rather than said). `--no-cut-pauses` turns it off.
  - On 197 judged keepers, the median length went from 41.8 s to 38.1 s.
  - The cuts are saved, so review shows them and can toggle them.
- **One bar in `review`**
  ([ADR 0031](../decisions/0031-review-editor-redesign.md) amendment,
  #280).
  - The rows of buttons above and below the film strip are one bar:
    view, trim, a Cuts menu, To pad, a Block menu and Snap. Below the
    strip there are only the lanes, named on the left.
  - "On screen" is a dropdown checklist of people.
- **A bin beside the timeline** (ADR 0031 amendment, #282). The pad and
  the B-roll sit in a tabbed bin to the timeline's left, next to the
  lanes they are dragged onto. Clear pad starts an edit empty.
- **A clip library**
  ([ADR 0035](../decisions/0035-clip-library.md), #284, #285).
  - Folders under `data/broll/`, in the bin's Library tab.
  - New folder makes one; To library copies a saved clip into it, named
    after its title.
  - From any other video's drafts, drag a library clip onto the Sequence
    lane: it goes in whole, with its own captions and sound.
- **Clips handed to DaVinci Resolve**
  ([ADR 0036](../decisions/0036-resolve-handoff.md), #288).
  - `clipper resolve "<clip title>" ["<another>" ...]` writes
    `data/clips/resolve/<title>/` with an FCPXML timeline, 1080x1920 at
    60 fps, from clips in any videos.
  - Each clip's kept stretches of its source video are separate timeline
    clips, so a cut can be opened back up (Trim mode in Resolve). The
    footage fills the frame's height; B-roll inserts are fitted.
  - The captions come as subtitles (`captions.srt`), with your caption
    fixes; the import steps are printed and saved.
  - Nothing is rendered: the timeline points at the videos in place.
  - On-screen texts, zooms, music and effects stay in review.
- **Fewer clicks in `review`** (ADR 0031 amendments, #259 to #270).
  - Sound effect markers can be dragged to move them, pressed to select,
    and removed with `Backspace`.
  - A sound palette: a button per sound adds it at the playhead, and `E`
    repeats the last sound and level from any tab.
  - A clip with no verdict starts with the people of the last clip saved
    from the same video.
  - After a word fix or hide, an offer to do the same to the span's
    other words that read the same.
  - A text after the first starts at the playhead for 3 s, in the last
    text's look.
  - `Alt+1` to `Alt+5` open the inspector's tabs.

Install from source. See the [README](../../README.md) for requirements and
usage.

## Also in this release

- `render --verbose` (`-v`) shows ffmpeg's full error log for a failed
  clip ([ADR 0011](../decisions/0011-render-command-shape-and-failure-policy.md)
  amendment, #291).
- With captions or titles on, `render` and `drafts` check once that
  ffmpeg has its `subtitles` filter (libass), and stop before cutting, or
  before any download, if not
  ([ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-burn-in-captions-from-segment-text.md)
  and 0011 amendments, #292).
- A reply from Ollama cut off at the length limit says so, instead of
  "not a valid score" ([ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-local-llm-detector-via-ollama.md)
  amendment, #293).
- `eval`'s closing note no longer says "one labeler", which was wrong for
  Twitch clip labels (#294).
- The Fortnite creators campaign is in `data/campaigns.txt` (#261).
- **Measured and not adopted**, each recorded so it is not repeated by
  accident:
  - **30 s windows** for the LLM detector, against 45 s, on four VODs at
    top 10, with 25 new drafts judged: keepers 10 to 12, but rated `good`
    5 to 1, and one keeper's end had to move 55 s. Windows stay 45 s
    ([ADR 0023](../decisions/0023-candidate-boundaries-follow-the-moment.md)
    and 0014 amendments, #287).
  - **A cold open**: the maintainer's marked peaks on 15 saved clips were
    all 1 to 9 s in, and neither the model naming the punchline nor the
    loudest 2.5 s found them more often than opening at 1 s
    ([ADR 0034](../decisions/0034-sequence-track-and-pad.md) amendment,
    #279).
  - **The ranker, rerun** on 25 VODs with 542 keeper and 481 reject
    windows (about double V6's): held-out keeper over reject 0.59 to
    0.60, as before. Of the first trash reasons, 12 of 16 are "nothing
    happening"
    ([ADR 0026](../decisions/0026-learn-from-judged-labels-then-performance.md)
    amendment, #290).
- `pyproject.toml` now says 0.8.0.
- New steps in the [manual test checklist](../manual-testing.md): P1,
  R19d, D14, C9, H1 and RV33 to RV41; R14 extended for `--verbose`.

## What was checked

CI runs ruff, mypy (strict) and pytest: 1,406 tests pass and 2 are
skipped. CI cannot run real ffmpeg, Whisper, yt-dlp, OpenCV's model,
Ollama, a browser or DaVinci Resolve.

The manual runs below were all on one machine: Windows 11, PowerShell 5.1,
ffmpeg `9.0.2-full_build-www.gyan.dev`, Ollama with `qwen3.5:9b` and
`nomic-embed-text`, and an RTX 4070 Ti with 12 GB.

- **Studio import, P1:** the assistant ran it on the maintainer's first
  export against a temporary posts file: 69 Shorts, 8 linked by the posts
  record, 7 matched, 54 not found (most titles were rewritten at upload).
- **Pause cuts, D14:** run in part by the assistant on an excerpt: three
  drafts of 41.5, 30.5 and 36.0 s after cuts.
- **The review page:** the assistant ran RV39 to RV41 in headless Edge
  over CDP, on scratch copies. A spliced clip with a library insert
  re-rendered to 76.75 s at 1080x1920. The maintainer has not yet run
  RV33 to RV41 in a browser.
- **Resolve, H1 and the spike:** the maintainer imported the spike's
  three hand-offs (finished pieces, the source timeline, subtitles) in
  DaVinci Resolve's free edition, then `clipper resolve`'s timeline: the
  footage filled the tall frame, a cut opened back up in Trim mode, the
  subtitles kept time, and GIF B-roll imported.
- **ffmpeg, F1, 2026-11-20:** with `-hide_banner -loglevel error`:
  - the no-audio run printed `Output file does not contain any stream`;
  - the invalid file printed `moov atom not found` and `Invalid data
    found when processing input`;
  - neither printed the banner.

  The runs without the flags printed the banner. All four runs exited
  non-zero.
- **Subtitles check:** `check_captions` found the filter on the real
  ffmpeg build. No build without libass was at hand, so C9's failing half
  has not been run.

These results were observed on one setup. They are not a guarantee for
other machines, builds or model versions.

## Known limitations

- **Picks read only the transcript**, and nothing learned or re-scored has
  beaten the LLM. On recent VODs its top 10 has often been 2 keepers to 8
  rejects. Expect to trash most of each top 10.
- **Most posted Shorts are not linked** to their drafts: titles rewritten
  at upload do not match. Record posts with `clipper posted` to link
  them.
- **The Resolve hand-off carries the cut and the captions' words only.**
  On-screen texts, zooms, crops, the face and facecam styles, overlays,
  music, effects and the captions' styling are redone in Resolve. A 16:9
  source loses its sides.
- **A library clip is a finished file.** It cannot be re-trimmed or
  re-captioned inside another clip; edit it in its own folder and copy
  it again.
- **The live preview is close, not exact.**
- **Titles and screen hooks still need checking:** swear words,
  misstated speakers, and "he" where a name was wanted.
- Still from earlier releases:
  - the LLM's ends are not extended;
  - facecam needs a bordered cam;
  - the `face` style follows the largest face;
  - letterboxed sources keep their bars;
  - yt-dlp warns on YouTube that it has no JavaScript runtime.
