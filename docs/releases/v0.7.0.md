# Clipper v0.7.0

Seventh release. The review page is now a one-screen editor. It shows a
live 9:16 preview, has keys for every frequent action and precision aids
for trimming. It edits captions, on-screen text, zooms and crops, audio,
B-roll and the order of a clip's parts, and it can join a VOD's clips or
tell its story as one longer Short. A `facecam` vertical style stacks a
gaming stream's cam box over the gameplay. The finer LLM score and a
letterbox fix were measured and not adopted. Everything still runs locally
and costs nothing.

## What it does

- **A one-screen editor in `review`**
  ([ADR 0031](../decisions/0031-review-editor-redesign.md), #214, #215).
  - The page fits one screen: the clip list, a preview, an inspector with
    tabs (Clip, Captions, Text, Frame, Audio) and a timeline across the
    bottom.
  - Below 1200 px wide, these stack.
- **Keys** (#216).
  - Play, J/K/L, frame step, start and end, cuts, rating, Save, Trash, and
    next and previous clip each have a key; `?` lists them.
  - After Save or Trash the page moves on to the next clip without a
    verdict. A box turns this off.
- **Precision aids** (#217).
  - A waveform from ffmpeg peaks, cached; the first one on a long VOD
    takes a couple of minutes.
  - A lane showing the words.
  - Handles and cuts snap to word edges within 0.3 s. Alt while dragging,
    or a toggle, turns snapping off.
  - Frames at a quarter of a second.
- **Live preview** (#218).
  - The clip is drawn by the browser as it will be rendered: through its
    cuts, framed as the chosen style, with captions, texts and zooms.
  - Save's re-render is still the exact result.
- **Caption editing**
  ([ADR 0017](../decisions/0017-word-timestamps-and-styled-captions.md)
  amendment, #219). Fix a word's text (its timing is kept), hide words,
  mark key words, and pick a highlight colour from five.
- **Text styling**
  ([ADR 0030](../decisions/0030-on-screen-text-and-thumbnails.md)
  amendment, #220).
  - Per on-screen text: a colour of six, a box or an outline, three sizes
    and a fade.
  - The text can be dragged anywhere on the preview.
  - Wrapped text now sits on one box (#211).
- **Zooms and hand crops**
  ([ADR 0016](../decisions/0016-vertical-centre-crop.md) and
  [0025](../decisions/0025-face-crop-style-and-picking-styles.md)
  amendments, #221).
  - Zooms: punch-ins of 1.1 to 2.0x over a stretch, cut in or smooth.
  - Hand crops: a 9:16 window placed by hand over a stretch.
  - Both are vertical only and are drawn on the preview.
- **Audio** ([ADR 0007](../decisions/0007-cut-clips-by-re-encoding.md) amendment,
  #223).
  - Clip volume.
  - A music bed from `data/music/`, lowered under speech.
  - Sound effects from `data/sfx/`.
  - The music and effects are your own files, mixed in the same encode.
- **Combine**
  ([ADR 0032](../decisions/0032-summarize-a-vods-clips.md), #225, #226,
  #228).
  - Joins a VOD's saved clips, as edited and in VOD order, into
    `<video name>_combined.mp4`.
  - Optional cards between clips and music.
  - A warning past 180 s.
  - Its own title (Suggest asks the local model), rating, thumbnail and
    trash.
- **Summarize**
  ([ADR 0033](../decisions/0033-summarize-a-vods-story.md), #229, #230,
  #231, #232).
  - The local model reads the whole transcript and picks the stream's
    story in 8 to 10 beats of 15 to 25 s, fitted to 170 s, with a card in
    its own words for each.
  - You edit the beats in a dialog and render them as
    `<video name>_summary.mp4`.
  - About a minute per VOD.
- **A sequence track and a pad**
  ([ADR 0034](../decisions/0034-sequence-track-and-pad.md), #237 to
  #240, #244).
  - A clip can be a list of blocks in any order: ranges of the VOD,
    repeated if wanted. Split (`B`), duplicate (`Shift+D`), remove and
    move them on a Sequence lane.
  - A pad per drafts folder keeps ranges marked anywhere in the VOD
    (`Shift+P`) to drop into a clip.
  - Zooms, hand crops and sound effects can apply to one block only.
  - The judged label's span stays the moment, so `eval` is unchanged.
- **B-roll** (ADR 0034 amendments, #241, #242, #243).
  - Your own videos and stills from `data/broll/` (`CLIPPER_BROLL_DIR`),
    used in two ways:
    - inserted as a block, with its own sound or silence;
    - laid over the clip as a full-frame cutaway or a picture-in-picture
      box dragged on the preview, while the clip's sound continues.
- **The `facecam` style**
  ([ADR 0025](../decisions/0025-face-crop-style-and-picking-styles.md)
  amendments, #233, #251, #252, #253).
  - Turn it on with `render --vertical --style facecam` or
    `drafts --style facecam`.
  - The cam box is found from the footage: a face that stays put, inside
    lasting straight borders. The cam is shown at full width over a centre
    crop of the gameplay, or under it with `--cam bottom`.
  - A clip with no box found renders in `fit`, with a note.
  - In `review`: Facecam sits in the style switch with a cam toggle.
    Save re-renders it, finding the box again, and records `cam` on the
    label.
  - With the cam at the bottom, captions sit just above it.
  - The style has no zooms, hand crops, sequence or B-roll.
- **Overlap and manual cuts in `review`**
  ([ADR 0024](../decisions/0024-review-drafts-in-a-local-browser-page.md)
  and [0029](../decisions/0029-cut-pauses-in-review.md) amendments,
  #208, #209, #210).
  - Save now refuses only an overlap that `eval` would count as a hit
    (half the shorter span), so two close keepers can both be saved.
  - Cuts can be marked by hand at the playhead (`[` and `]`), or by
    dragging a range on the film strip.
- **A reason on Trash** ([ADR 0012](../decisions/0012-evaluate-detectors-against-hand-labels.md)
  amendment, #234, #245).
  - While Trash is armed, keys `1` to `5` pick a reason: nothing
    happening, needs context, seen not said, bad cut, other.
  - The reason is saved on the rejected span. It is optional.
- **`posted` by title or relative path**
  ([ADR 0028](../decisions/0028-post-queue.md) amendments, #213, #227).
  - `clipper posted "<title>" <link>` takes the title as the queue shows
    it.
  - A path relative to the current folder or the clips dir also works.
  - Combined clips and summaries are queued and posted like clips.
- **Upload copies named after titles** (ADR 0028 amendment, V7 item 7h).
  - Each `queue` run puts a copy of every queued clip and its thumbnail
    in `<clips dir>/upload/`, named after the title, for example
    `ron-shocked-at-the-gas-pump.mp4`. `queue.md` points at these copies.
  - The copy is a hard link where the drive allows one. Clipper's own file
    names are unchanged.

Install from source. See the [README](../../README.md) for requirements and
usage.

## Also in this release

- ffmpeg failures on Windows show the exit code signed, with a name for
  invalid data, stream not found and permission denied (`exit
  -1094995529, invalid data`) ([ADR 0004](../decisions/0004-report-ffmpeg-errors.md)
  amendment, #246).
- Titles no longer end with emoji: the prompt is back to "no emoji",
  and you add them in review where wanted. On the 110 keepers measured
  in V6, that wording named the person in 68 titles, against 61 with emoji
  ([ADR 0018](../decisions/0018-suggested-titles-via-ollama.md)
  amendment, V7 item 7i).
- `render`'s message for a malformed analysis file says "Invalid JSON"
  once (#247).
- The review page reads a refused oversized request body before it
  answers 400 (#224).
- `OllamaClient` moved to a shared adapter with its own error. This
  changed no behaviour (#212).
- `docs/architecture.md` describes the current layers again. Duplicate
  `.gitignore` entries were removed (#249).
- **Measured and not adopted**, each recorded so it is not repeated by
  accident:
  - **A finer LLM score.** A 0 to 9 rubric and Ollama's token
    probabilities were tried on ten VODs at top 10. The rubric's
    probabilities tied the current prompt at 70 keepers and 30 rejects;
    the other variants did worse. The 69 newly judged windows are
    committed ([ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-local-llm-detector-via-ollama.md)
    amendment, #236).
  - **Letterboxed sources** under `--vertical`. `cropdetect` found
    `test_input.mp4`'s bars but none on 30 real VODs, and it reported
    large false boxes in dark scenes. A rule is recorded for when a
    letterboxed source turns up (ADR 0016 amendment, #250).
- Saving yt-dlp's video info in the download request is parked, with what
  it would take (#248).
- `pyproject.toml` now says 0.7.0.
- New steps in the [manual test checklist](../manual-testing.md): R21 to
  R23, Q3, Q4, RV15 to RV28, RV29a, RV29b and RV30 to RV32.

## What was checked

CI runs ruff, mypy (strict) and pytest: 1,317 tests pass and 2 are
skipped. CI cannot run real ffmpeg, Whisper, yt-dlp, OpenCV's model,
Ollama or a browser.

The manual runs below were all on one machine: Windows 11, PowerShell 5.1,
ffmpeg `9.0.2-full_build-www.gyan.dev`, Ollama 0.40.13 with `qwen3.5:9b`,
yt-dlp 2026.10.26, and an RTX 4070 Ti with 12 GB.

- **The review page:** the assistant ran RV27 to RV32 in headless Edge
  over CDP, on scratch copies of drafts folders and labels. The maintainer
  has not yet run RV17 to RV32 in a browser.
- **Renders:** the assistant ran R21 (a sequence), R22 (B-roll) and R23
  (facecam) and checked frames of the output by eye.
- **Facecam:** in the spike, a bordered cam was boxed right on 18 of 20
  stretches and none was wrong; no box was found on 12 stretches without
  a cam. A borderless cam whose layout changes was mostly missed.
- **ffmpeg, F1, 2026-11-16:** with `-hide_banner -loglevel error`:
  - the no-audio run printed `Output file does not contain any stream`;
  - the invalid file printed `moov atom not found` and `Invalid data
    found when processing input`;
  - neither printed the banner.

  The runs without the flags printed the banner. All four runs exited
  non-zero (-22 and -1094995529).

These results were observed on one setup. They are not a guarantee for
other machines, builds or model versions.

## Known limitations

- **Picks read only the transcript**, and nothing learned or re-scored has
  beaten the LLM yet. Expect to trash a third or more of each top 10.
- **The LLM's ends are not extended.** Expect to move ends in `review`.
- **The live preview is close, not exact.** Font sizes, line breaks and
  colour emoji can differ from Save's render.
- **Facecam needs a bordered cam.** A cam without a border, or a layout
  that changes during the clip, usually renders as `fit`. The facecam
  style has no zooms, hand crops, sequence or B-roll, and its thumbnails
  are framed as `fit`.
- **Wrapped on-screen text needs libass 0.17 or newer** for its single
  box. Only one ffmpeg build has been tried.
- **The remux is unchecked on a real late download** (D13).
- **Posted Shorts are not linked to how they did.** The CSV import and
  report are for a later version.
- **Titles still need checking:** swear words and misstated speakers
  remain.
- Still from earlier releases:
  - the `face` style follows the largest face;
  - letterboxed sources keep their bars;
  - there is no early libass check;
  - `render` has no `-v`;
  - yt-dlp warns on YouTube that it has no JavaScript runtime.

## Pinned dependencies

Unchanged: `av==17.0.0`, `huggingface-hub<1.0` and
`opencv-python-headless>=5.0.0.93,<6`. No dependency was added. The
facecam finder uses OpenCV and numpy, which were already present. See
[ADR 0003](../decisions/0003-pin-transcription-dependencies.md) before
unpinning any of them.
