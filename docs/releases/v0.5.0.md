# Clipper v0.5.0

Fifth release. Drafts can now be judged and fixed in a local review page,
and those judgments are what picking changes are measured against. `drafts`
takes a list of sources, uses the LLM detector and the top 10 by default,
renders a face-following crop beside the fitted one, and writes titles that
know the channel, the video and the people in it. Everything still runs
locally and costs nothing.

## What it does

- **`clipper review <drafts folder>`** opens a page on 127.0.0.1 (`--port
  8765`) with the clip list, a player over the whole source video, a film
  strip with start and end handles, and title and notes boxes
  ([ADR 0024](../decisions/0024-review-drafts-in-a-local-browser-page.md),
  #130, #132, #133, #135, #137, #139, #140, #142, #143, #167). Save writes
  a judged label to `<labels dir>/picks/<video name>.json` and re-renders
  the clip when its span moved (about 12 s), with the folder's flags; Trash
  deletes the file and writes a rejected span; "Add clip here" saves a
  moment no draft proposed. Each draft plays with a button per style, and
  Save keeps one. Reset restores the draft's span and the model's title.
  Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the standard library's HTTP server; no
  build step.
- **`eval` reads judged labels**
  ([ADR 0012](../decisions/0012-evaluate-detectors-against-hand-labels.md),
  #128, #144): verdicts `post`, `fix` (with the draft's span) and `added`,
  rejected spans, and per candidate whether it sits on a keeper, a rejected
  span or nothing judged yet. It reports keepers that end early or start
  late by more than 1 s, and the gap at each end.
- **`drafts` takes several sources or `--list <file>`**, one per line, `#`
  comments, `| about text` per line
  ([ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-one-command-to-drafts.md), #169). Every
  source is checked before any runs; a failed source does not stop the
  rest; a summary table ends the run, and the exit code is 1 if any failed.
- **`drafts` defaults to the LLM detector and the top 10** (`--top N`),
  from the judged baseline: 18 keepers at top 10 on three VODs against the
  heuristic's 12
  ([ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-local-llm-detector-via-ollama.md), #171).
  `analyze` and `eval` keep the heuristic and the top 5. `drafts` now needs
  Ollama running even with `--no-titles`, unless `--detector heuristic`.
- **A `face` vertical style**
  ([ADR 0025](../decisions/0025-face-crop-style-and-picking-styles.md),
  #159, #160, #162, #164, #165). `render --vertical --style face` crops the
  full height around the largest face, found by OpenCV's YuNet model 5
  times a second and cached under `<cache dir>/faces/`. The crop holds one
  position per shot and moves only at a cut or after 2 s away. `drafts`
  renders both `fit` and `face` (`--style` to choose), as `<name>.mp4` and
  `<name>.face.mp4`; a clip with no face is a note, and still renders in
  `fit`.
- **Titles know who is on screen**
  ([ADR 0018](../decisions/0018-suggested-titles-via-ollama.md), #152,
  #154, #156, #173). `fetch` saves `<id>.info.json` (title, channel, date,
  duration, description) beside the video, for a reused file too, and the
  title prompt gets the channel and video title. `--about "<text>"` on
  `render` and `drafts` adds names and context by hand, for local files
  too. Titles use he or she only when `--about` states pronouns
  ("Lacy (he/him)"); otherwise they repeat the name. On the reviewed
  keepers, titles naming the person went from 1 of 39 with no context to 12
  with video info and 22 with `--about`.
- **Candidate boundaries**
  ([ADR 0023](../decisions/0023-candidate-boundaries-follow-the-moment.md),
  #146, #148). Windows under 15 s are no longer scored, for both
  detectors. The heuristic extends each end to the next 1.5 s pause in
  speech, up to 90 s and never into the next chosen candidate: on the
  baseline its early ends went from 6 to 1. The LLM does not extend, since
  it did not help there.
- **`clipper labels <links file>`** writes a labels file from
  hand-collected Twitch clip links, reading their metadata with yt-dlp
  (ADR 0012 and 0020 amendments, #116, #117).

Install from source. See the [README](../../README.md) for requirements and
usage.

## Also in this release

- Titles are limited to 8 words and hooks to 15 in the prompt, with no
  emoji or swear words; on 35 clips no title ran over 60 characters (#114).
- `review` refuses a video whose timestamps do not start at 0 and names the
  lossless remux: one Twitch download started at 64 s, and the browser's
  player ran 64 s behind the transcript (#143).
- Film strip frames are named by video, so the browser no longer shows one
  VOD's cached frames in another's strip (#142).
- `pyproject.toml` now says 0.5.0; it had said 0.2.0 since v0.2.0.
- **Measured and not adopted**, each recorded so it is not repeated by
  accident:
  - Merging neighbouring windows into one candidate: of five neighbouring
    pairs in the top 10s, none would gain (ADR 0023 item 7 amendment,
    #150).
  - Audio loudness as a detector input: keepers against rejected spans sat
    inside chance on five VODs (AUC 0.52 to 0.58), though community clips
    lean loud (#175).
  - Whisper `small` on the CPU: on one VOD it took 1.8x as long per hour of
    video, wrote half the periods, and the LLM's top 10 lost all 5 known
    keepers ([ADR 0021](../decisions/0021-gpu-transcription-measured-not-adopted.md)
    amendment, #177).
- Judged labels for 11 videos are committed in `data/labels/picks/`, with
  the V5 baseline (#145) and later runs (#151, #176, #178).
- The [manual test checklist](../manual-testing.md) gained a Review page
  section (RV0 to RV10), a Labels section (T0 to T6), R19a, R19b and R20,
  D10a and D12.

## What was checked

CI runs ruff, mypy (strict) and pytest: 912 tests pass and 2 are skipped.
It cannot run real ffmpeg, Whisper, yt-dlp, OpenCV's model, Ollama or a
browser. These manual runs were on one machine: Windows 11, PowerShell 5.1,
ffmpeg `9.0.2-full_build-www.gyan.dev`, Ollama 0.40.13 with `qwen3.5:9b`,
yt-dlp 2026.10.26, RTX 4070 Ti 12 GB.

- **Daily use, 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-10:** `drafts` on 11 videos (Twitch
  VODs, a YouTube stream, IRL videos and a podcast), every draft judged in
  `review`. The baseline is under "V5 baseline" in `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Review page:** RV1 to RV9 passed, run by the maintainer in a browser;
  RV2, RV3 and RV5's timings were also run by the assistant without one.
  **RV10 (the style switch) was not run as written.** The switch was used
  on five drafts folders, where all 35 keepers were saved with a style.
- **Titles:** R19a and R19b measured on the reviewed keepers (counts
  above).
- **Face style:** R20; the maintainer's viewing found the first crop
  choppy, and the locked framing replaced it (#164).
- **Fetch:** D10a in part; the info file is written for Twitch and
  YouTube, and the yt-dlp-missing warning was not run. D12 (lists) was not
  run as written.
- **ffmpeg, F1, 2026-11-10:** with `-hide_banner -loglevel error`, the
  no-audio run printed `Output file does not contain any stream` and the
  invalid file printed `moov atom not found` and `Invalid data found when
  processing input`, with no banner; the runs without the flags printed the
  banner. All four exited non-zero.

That is what was observed on one setup, not a guarantee for other machines,
builds or model versions.

## Known limitations

- **Picks read only the transcript.** About half of the moments worth
  clipping are visual. On three trial VODs, the LLM's top 10s gave 18 keepers
  of 30 and the heuristic's 12.
- **The LLM's ends are not extended**, and on the baseline 6 of its 18
  keepers ended early. Expect to move ends in `review`.
- **The `face` style crops on the largest face**, which is sometimes the
  listener in a two-shot. In the judged runs every keeper was saved in
  `fit`.
- **`drafts` took 15 to 17 minutes per VOD** in the baseline, with the LLM
  and a cached transcript, before both styles were rendered; face tracking
  adds to that, untimed. A 5-hour stream's first run adds about 21 minutes
  of CPU transcription.
- **A download whose timestamps do not start at 0** (seen once, on a
  Twitch VOD) is refused by `review` until remuxed by hand.
- **Titles still need checking:** one clip keeps a swear word under every
  prompt tried, some hooks misstate the speaker, and the pronoun rule is
  not checked on the reply.
- Still from earlier releases: letterboxed sources keep their bars, no
  early libass check, no `-v` on `render`, unsigned ffmpeg exit codes on
  Windows, and yt-dlp's JavaScript runtime warning on YouTube.

## Pinned dependencies

`opencv-python-headless>=5.0.0.93,<6` is added; 4.x would pull `numpy`
down. The YuNet face model ships in the package with its MIT licence, and
nothing is downloaded at run time. `av==17.0.0` and `huggingface-hub<1.0`
are unchanged. See
[ADR 0003](../decisions/0003-pin-transcription-dependencies.md) before
unpinning any of them.

## Not in this release

Posting or uploads, analytics or platform metrics, hosted APIs or paid
services, the movies/TV niche, tuning for education or podcasts, a URL box
in the review page, channel watching or scheduling, audience signals as a
detector input, the Twitch API, a trained model, the facecam split, speaker
detection, a database and user accounts.
