# Clipper v0.6.0

Sixth release. The review page now does the editing that used to happen
after it: cutting pauses, putting text on screen and making a thumbnail.
Kept clips are rated and lined up in a post queue with their titles,
captions and campaign tags, and drafts are filed by channel and day.
Titles use a saved description of each channel and end with emoji. Three
learning experiments were measured and none was adopted; their numbers
are recorded so they are not repeated by accident. Everything still runs
locally and costs nothing.

## What it does

- **Cut silence in `review`**
  ([ADR 0029](../decisions/0029-cut-pauses-in-review.md), #192, #194,
  #195, #196, #197). "Cut silence" proposes cutting every pause in speech
  of 0.6 s or more, keeping 0.15 s either side; each cut toggles on or off
  in the film strip, and Preview plays the clip with the cuts skipped.
  Save renders the kept parts joined in the one re-encode, with captions
  and the face crop shifted to match, and stores the cuts on the judged
  label and the manifest. The clip's span, and so `eval`, is unchanged.
- **On-screen text in `review`**
  ([ADR 0030](../decisions/0030-on-screen-text-and-thumbnails.md), #198,
  #199, #200). Up to 5 text boxes per clip, each with its words, a start
  and end in the finished clip's seconds, and `top` or `middle`; the first
  starts as the title, from 0 to 3 s at the top. Bold white on a
  half-dark box, burned in with the captions in the same encode, with or
  without word captions. Unsaved text is drawn over the draft player.
- **Thumbnails in `review`** (ADR 0030, #201). "Make thumbnail" saves the
  playhead's frame in the style shown, without captions, with optional
  text, as `<clip name>.thumb.jpg` beside the clip, for YouTube Studio's
  custom thumbnail. Trash deletes it; the queue lists it.
- **Rating and people in `review`**
  ([ADR 0024](../decisions/0024-review-drafts-in-a-local-browser-page.md)
  amendment, #190). Save may mark a keeper `good` (to post) or `ok` (for
  the backlog) and who is on screen, from `data/people.txt`
  (`CLIPPER_PEOPLE_FILE`). Both go on the judged label.
- **`clipper queue` and `clipper posted`**
  ([ADR 0028](../decisions/0028-post-queue.md), #191, #193). `queue` lists
  saved `good` clips not yet posted, one drafts folder at a time in turn
  (newest first), two a day (`--per-day`, `--start`, `--include-ok`), and
  writes `<clips dir>/queue.md` with each clip's file, title and caption in
  code blocks, its thumbnail, people and the campaigns it qualifies for
  (`data/campaigns.txt`: name, hashtag, minimum people, people, optional
  channels). `clipper posted <folder>/<file> <YouTube link>` records the
  post in `data/posts.json`, and the clip leaves the queue.
- **Drafts filed by channel and day**
  ([ADR 0019](../decisions/0019-a-folder-per-video.md)
  amendment, #193). `drafts` writes to
  `<clips dir>/<channel>/<MM-DD-YYYY>/<video name>/`, the day the drafts
  ran, with an existing folder for the video reused. `review`, `queue` and
  `posted` find the nested folders; `posted` takes a full path or
  `<video name>/<file>`.
- **Titles use a saved about text per channel**
  ([ADR 0018](../decisions/0018-suggested-titles-via-ollama.md)
  amendments, #185, #186). `data/channels.txt` (`CLIPPER_CHANNELS_FILE`)
  holds `<channel> | <about text>` lines, used when no `--about` is given
  and the video has an info file; the run prints `About (saved for
  <channel>)`. On 110 reviewed keepers, titles naming the person went from
  48 to 68. Titles now end with one or two fitting emoji, at the
  maintainer's choice; naming the person went from 68 to 61 with them.
- **`fetch` remuxes a download whose timestamps start late**
  ([ADR 0020](../decisions/0020-fetch-a-video-by-url.md) amendment,
  #203). A new or reused file starting more than 0.1 s in is rewritten in
  place without re-encoding, so `review` opens it; `review`'s refusal now
  says to fetch again.

Install from source. See the [README](../../README.md) for requirements and
usage.

## Also in this release

- The review page's start and end handles were missing after the cuts
  change; a second function with the same name hid the first. A test now
  checks the page's function names are unique (#195).
- **Measured and not adopted**, each recorded so it is not repeated by
  accident:
  - The maintainer's own titles as examples in the title prompt: on 110
    keepers, titles naming the person went from 48 to 32, and the model
    copied other videos' people and tone (ADR 0018 amendment, #184).
  - Judged keepers and rejects from other VODs as examples in the LLM
    detector's prompt: on four VODs at top 10, keepers 29 to 25 and
    rejects 11 to 12 ([ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-local-llm-detector-via-ollama.md)
    amendment, #189).
  - A trained ranker on text embeddings (`nomic-embed-text`) and the
    heuristic's signals, leave one VOD out on 15 VODs: it put a keeper
    above a reject 59 to 61% of the time, and its own top 10s were almost
    all moments never judged, so its picks could not be scored. Embedding
    is fast (15 VODs in about 1.5 minutes); a rerun waits for about twice
    the labels ([ADR 0026](../decisions/0026-learn-from-judged-labels-then-performance.md)
    amendment, #204).
- ADRs 0026 (learning from judged labels, then performance) and 0027
  (performance data from a hand-exported YouTube Studio CSV) are decided;
  the CSV import is not built (#182).
- Judged labels for more videos are committed in `data/labels/picks/`
  (#187, #189, #202), and the CORE and JasonTheWeen campaign briefs are
  recorded (#188).
- `pyproject.toml` now says 0.6.0.
- The [manual test checklist](../manual-testing.md) gained R19c, D13, Q1,
  Q2 and RV11 to RV14.

## What was checked

CI runs ruff, mypy (strict) and pytest: 1,059 tests pass and 2 are
skipped. It cannot run real ffmpeg, Whisper, yt-dlp, OpenCV's model,
Ollama or a browser. These manual runs were on one machine: Windows 11,
PowerShell 5.1, ffmpeg `9.0.2-full_build-www.gyan.dev`, Ollama 0.40.13
with `qwen3.5:9b` and `nomic-embed-text`, yt-dlp 2026.10.26, RTX 4070 Ti
12 GB.

- **Daily use, 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-12:** `drafts`, `review` with
  ratings and people, and `queue` on the maintainer's posting runs.
- **Review page:** RV12 (cuts), RV13 (on-screen text) and RV14
  (thumbnails, including the queue's thumbnail line) passed, run by the
  maintainer in a browser. RV11, Q1 and Q2 were exercised in daily use and
  by the assistant's checks, not recorded as run step by step.
- **Titles:** R19c and the emoji change measured on the 110 reviewed
  keepers (counts above).
- **Fetch:** D13 was not run: no download starting late was at hand (the
  one seen had already been remuxed by hand). On `test_input.mp4` the
  remuxer kept the duration and put the index first.
- **ffmpeg, F1, 2026-11-12:** with `-hide_banner -loglevel error`, the
  no-audio run printed `Output file does not contain any stream` and the
  invalid file printed `moov atom not found` and `Invalid data found when
  processing input`, with no banner; the runs without the flags printed the
  banner. All four exited non-zero.

That is what was observed on one setup, not a guarantee for other machines,
builds or model versions.

## Known limitations

- **Picks read only the transcript**, and nothing learned has beaten the
  LLM yet. Expect to trash a third or more of each top 10.
- **The LLM's ends are not extended.** Expect to move ends in `review`.
- **Cuts are pauses only.** There are no hand-drawn cuts yet, and a cut
  between words can clip a breath or a laugh; toggle it off.
- **Emoji in on-screen text render in one colour**, through font fallback.
- **`review` refuses a span that overlaps another clip's label**, even
  slightly, so two close keepers cannot both be saved.
- **The remux is unchecked on a real late download** (D13).
- **Posted Shorts are not linked to how they did.** `posted` records the
  link; the CSV import and report are for a later version.
- **Titles still need checking:** swear words and misstated speakers
  remain, and emoji lowered how often a title names the person.
- Still from earlier releases: the `face` style follows the largest face,
  letterboxed sources keep their bars, no early libass check, no `-v` on
  `render`, unsigned ffmpeg exit codes on Windows, and yt-dlp's JavaScript
  runtime warning on YouTube.

## Pinned dependencies

Unchanged: `av==17.0.0`, `huggingface-hub<1.0` and
`opencv-python-headless>=5.0.0.93,<6`. No dependency was added; the ranker
spike used Ollama and numpy, already present. See
[ADR 0003](../decisions/0003-pin-transcription-dependencies.md) before
unpinning any of them.

## Not in this release

Posting or uploads, the YouTube or Twitch APIs, OAuth or logins, the
YouTube Studio CSV import and performance report, a trained ranker or
retraining command, hosted or paid models, fine-tuning, audio or frame
embeddings, the facecam split, speaker detection, the movies/TV niche, a
URL box in the review page, channel watching or scheduling, a database and
user accounts.
